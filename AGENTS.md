## Stays on this Mac

`calls/` (call audio and transcripts) and `from-customer/` (files the customer sent) are in
`.gitignore` and never leave this Mac. A summary of them may be committed; the files may not.

## Customer-facing files

The intro, portfolio and presenter never carry: internal product names (Open*), the budget,
the current employer, the first customer's name, or personal motives. Screens show example
data only (가람상사 and its cast). Prices and terms are TY's decision; leave a `[ ]` blank.
